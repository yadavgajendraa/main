Research

I study how to detect and quantify physical changes in structures from their measured vibrations. My work combines stochastic subspace system identification (SSI) of operational data with physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), so that identified modal parameters can be tied back to physical quantities such as stiffness and mass.

Current threads: hybrid PINN-based parameter identification, physics-aware networks coupled with SSI, and turning shifts in identified modal parameters into damage indicators for bridges.

Demo: from damage to alarm

A finite element model of a simply supported girder (20 beam elements). Damage is a local loss of bending stiffness. It lowers the natural frequencies, and the ratio of damaged to healthy frequency is the health index of each mode. When an index falls below 0.98, the monitor raises an alert. Click the girder to move the damage, then press Drive vehicle across: a moving load crosses the span, an accelerometer at the sensor (choose its position with the slider) records the response, and the frequencies are picked from its spectrum. The health index below comes from that simulated measurement.
